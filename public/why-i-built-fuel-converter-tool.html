Why I Built Fuel Converter Tool

Like many drivers, I use a fuel-tracking app to monitor fuel economy and keep detailed records of my vehicle's fuel purchases. I enjoy seeing how fuel economy changes over time and keeping accurate records of each fill-up.

Everything worked perfectly until I started traveling across the border into Canada.

When driving in Canada, I often switch my vehicle's display from miles to kilometers so it's easier to read speed limits and road signs. That sounds simple enough until it's time to buy fuel.

Then I pull up to the fuel pump and of course fuel is sold in liters and priced in Canadian dollars. My fuel-tracking app still expects miles, gallons, and U.S. dollars. Before I can enter a single fuel purchase, I have to convert the odometer reading, fuel price, and fuel amount into the format my fuel-tracking app expects.

Every fill-up became a small math project.

I found myself writing numbers down, trying to remember what needed to be converted later, or pulling out my phone and doing multiple conversions before I could enter a single fuel purchase into my app.

The Search for a Solution

I looked for a simple tool that could handle everything in one place: odometer readings, fuel amounts, fuel prices, and currency conversions. I even tried using AI to help with the conversions, but it still wasn't giving me the exact format I needed for my fuel-tracking app.

I found plenty of individual conversion calculators, but I couldn't find a tool designed specifically for fuel-tracking users who travel between countries and need to convert odometer readings, fuel amounts, fuel prices, and currency values in one place.

Most solutions required multiple calculators, multiple browser tabs, or more manual work than I wanted to do while standing at a fuel pump.

Building Fuel Converter Tool

Eventually I decided to build the tool I wished existed.

Fuel Converter Tool allows drivers to enter fuel purchase information exactly as it appears and convert it into the format required by their preferred fuel-tracking app.

The goal was simple: reduce the time spent doing conversions and reduce the chance of making mistakes when recording fuel purchases.

Who This Tool Is For

Fuel Converter Tool was built for drivers who care about maintaining accurate fuel records, especially those who travel between the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

Whether you use Fuelly, Fuelio, Drivvo, Simply Auto, or another fuel-tracking app, the goal is the same: accurate records with less hassle.

If Fuel Converter Tool saves you a few minutes and a little frustration on your next fuel stop, then it has done exactly what it was built to do.
